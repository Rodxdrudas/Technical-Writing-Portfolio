AI-Assisted Campaign Creation & Knowledge Management Playbook

Version: 1.0
Document Owner: Rodolfo Esteves
Audience: Marketing Teams, CRM Teams, Campaign Managers, Marketing Operations, Analytics Teams

### Executive Summary

This playbook provides guidance for using Artificial Intelligence (AI) to support marketing campaign planning, audience management, content creation, reporting, and knowledge sharing.

The objective is to:

Improve campaign efficiency

Standardize AI usage

Reduce repetitive tasks

Improve knowledge accessibility

Support responsible AI adoption

1. Purpose

AI can assist marketing teams throughout the campaign lifecycle, from planning and audience creation to reporting and optimization.

This document defines:

Recommended AI use cases

Governance standards

Prompting best practices

Knowledge management processes

Quality assurance requirements

3. Responsible AI Principles

#### Human Oversight

AI-generated outputs must be reviewed before use.

AI recommendations should support decision-making but should not replace business judgment.

#### Accuracy Verification

Always validate:

Product information

Campaign rules

Audience definitions

Regulatory requirements

#### Privacy Protection

Never enter:

Customer names

Personal email addresses

Phone numbers

Sensitive personal information

into AI systems unless approved by company policy.

11. Best Practices

#### Do

✅ Standardize prompts
✅ Maintain prompt repositories
✅ Document campaign learnings
✅ Review AI outputs
✅ Update knowledge assets regularly
✅ Track adoption metrics
#### Avoid

❌ Publishing AI content without validation
❌ Storing undocumented prompts
❌ Using customer-identifiable information in prompts
❌ Creating duplicate documentation repositories
