1. Overview

### Purpose

This guide explains the process used to define, create, validate, and activate customer audiences for marketing campaigns.

The objective is to ensure that audience selection is accurate, compliant, measurable, and aligned with campaign objectives.

### Scope

This document covers:

* Business requirements gathering

* Audience definition

* Segmentation criteria

* Data validation

* Campaign activation

* Performance monitoring

* Common troubleshooting scenarios

5. Audience Definition

### Audience Design Principles

Every segment should be:

#### Specific

Avoid broad definitions.

Poor Example:

All customers

Improved Example:

Customers who purchased school-related products within the last 12 months and are subscribed to email communications.

#### Measurable

The audience size should be quantifiable.

Document:

Total population

Reachable population

Eligible population

#### Actionable

Every audience should support a business objective.

11. Common Issues

### Issue

Audience Size Unexpectedly Low

### Possible Causes

Restrictive filters

Missing consent records

Data synchronization issues

### Resolution

Review segment criteria and validate source data.

### Issue

Campaign Reach Lower Than Expected

### Possible Causes

High exclusion volume

Outdated customer permissions

Incomplete audience synchronization

### Resolution

Validate audience counts across source systems.

12. Best Practices

### Do

✅ Document all audience criteria
✅ Maintain version control
✅ Validate audience counts before activation
✅ Capture business justification for segmentation decisions
✅ Review campaign performance for future optimization

### Avoid

❌ Unclear audience definitions
❌ Missing exclusion criteria
❌ Launching without QA
❌ Using undocumented segment logic
