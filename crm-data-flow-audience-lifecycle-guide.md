1. Overview

### Purpose

This guide provides a structured overview of customer data movement across systems and explains how audiences progress from raw customer records to active marketing segments.

The objective is to ensure:

Data consistency

Audience accuracy

Regulatory compliance

Campaign effectiveness

Operational transparency

### Scope

This document covers:

Data sources

Customer data ingestion

Identity resolution

Audience creation

Audience activation

Performance feedback loops

Data governance considerations

2. Business Context

Typical business outcomes supported include:

Personalized communications

7. Audience Lifecycle

#### Stage 1 – Data Collection

Customer information enters the ecosystem through approved data collection mechanisms.

Examples

Newsletter subscriptions

Website registrations

Product purchases

Loyalty enrollment

#### Stage 2 – Data Validation

Customer records are evaluated for:

Data quality

Duplicate records

Consent status

Compliance requirements

#### Stage 3 – Profile Enrichment

Additional information may be appended to customer records.

Examples:

Behavioral attributes

Purchase history

Engagement scores

Customer lifetime value

#### Stage 4 – Audience Creation

Business rules are applied to define target segments.

Example Segment

High-Value Coffee Buyers

Criteria:

Country = Portugal

AND

Purchase Frequency > 4

AND

Category = Coffee

AND

Email Consent = True

Show more lines

#### Stage 5 – Audience Validation

Review:

✅ Audience size
✅ Business logic
✅ Exclusions
✅ Compliance requirements
✅ Data freshness
#### Stage 6 – Audience Activation

Approved segments are distributed to activation channels.

Channels

Email

SMS

Push Notifications

Paid Media Platforms

Website Personalization Engines

#### Stage 7 – Engagement Collection

Customer responses are captured.

Examples:

Opens

Clicks

Purchases

Redeemed Offers

App Usage

#### Stage 8 – Performance Analysis

Results are analyzed against campaign objectives.

Typical KPIs:

| Metric | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Open Rate | Email Opens |
| CTR | Click Through Rate |
| Conversion Rate | Completed Actions |
| Revenue | Sales Generated |
| Retention Rate | Repeat Activity |

#### Stage 9 – Optimization

Audience definitions are adjusted based on performance findings.

Examples:

Refine segmentation criteria

Adjust exclusions

Introduce new behavioral attributes

10. Common Issues & Resolutions

#### Issue

Audience Volume Too Small

#### Possible Causes

Overly restrictive filters

Missing source data

Expired permissions

#### Resolution

Review criteria and validate source systems.

#### Issue

Audience Counts Differ Between Systems

#### Possible Causes

Synchronization delays

Identity matching discrepancies

Data refresh timing

#### Resolution

Compare source timestamps and reconciliation reports.

#### Issue

Unexpected Campaign Performance

#### Possible Causes

Incorrect audience definition

Data quality issues

Incomplete activation

#### Resolution

Review segmentation logic and activation logs.

11. Best Practices

#### Do

✅ Use documented segmentation logic
✅ Validate audience counts
✅ Maintain version history
✅ Monitor data quality
✅ Review audience performance regularly
✅ Document all activation decisions
#### Avoid

❌ Undocumented audience changes
❌ Activating audiences without permission checks
❌ Using outdated customer data
❌ Creating duplicate audience definitions
12. Glossary

#### CRM

Customer Relationship Management system used to manage customer interactions and relationships.

#### CDP

Customer Data Platform that consolidates customer information from multiple systems into a unified profile.

### Identity Resolution

The process of matching and merging records belonging to the same customer.

#### Audience Segment

A defined customer group sharing common characteristics or behaviors.

#### Activation

The process of delivering audience segments to marketing execution platforms.

#### Customer Lifecycle

The series of stages a customer progresses through during their relationship with a brand.
